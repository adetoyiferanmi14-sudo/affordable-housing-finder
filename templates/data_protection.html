DATA PROTECTION

How we handle your data

The system minimises data, stores no identifiers, and processes inputs transiently. Here is exactly what that means.

What we ask for

To check eligibility we ask for your age, monthly income, NHF contribution months, years of Lagos residency, monthly loan repayments, prior homeownership and available equity. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any ID number. Age and income are personal information, so they are processed, but they are not linked to who you are. The repayments figure is used once to work out a debt-to-income ratio and is then discarded.

What is stored, and for how long

While you use the site, your answers are held in a temporary session record on the server. Your browser keeps only a random session code, in a secure cookie that scripts cannot read. The record contains no name or contact details. It expires after one hour, or when you close your browser, and expired records are removed by a routine clean-up task. Nothing else about your answers is kept.

Logs and third parties

Like any website, our hosting provider (PythonAnywhere) may keep server logs that include your IP address and the pages requested, for its own operational and security purposes. Fonts and some images may be loaded from third-party services (Google Fonts and Unsplash), which receive your IP address and browser details when a page loads. We use no analytics or advertising trackers and we do not share your eligibility answers with anyone.

Your rights under the NDPA

The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 gives you rights over personal data that identifies you, including the rights to be informed, to access and correct your data, to have it erased, and to object to its processing. Because your answers sit in a short-lived record that is not linked to your name or contact details, we cannot look it up for you afterwards. You can end it at any time by closing your browser. This is an academic demonstration system and its results are not official decisions.
