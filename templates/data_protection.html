DATA PROTECTION

How we handle your data

Your privacy matters. Here's how we collect, use, and protect your information on this site.

No personal data stored

This site does not collect or store any personal information. You do not need to create an account, enter your name, or provide an email address. The eligibility checker uses the details you enter during your visit, and those details are not saved anywhere after you close the page.

Session-only storage

The details you enter on the Eligibility Checker page are stored temporarily in your browser session so you can view matching properties without re-entering them. This session ends when you close your browser. No database record is created.

No third-party sharing

No data is shared with any third party. No analytics trackers. No advertising cookies. No external services receive any information about you.

Your rights under the NDPA

The Nigeria Data Protection Act grants you rights over your personal data. Because this system stores no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or erase. The system is designed to satisfy the NDPA by not collecting data in the first place.
